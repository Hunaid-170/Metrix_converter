Informative Articles on Measurement Units

Learn about SI units, metric systems, traditional units, and more.

SI System

The International System of Units (SI) is the modern form of the metric system, widely used in science and industry worldwide.

Metric System

A decimal-based system using units like meters, liters, and grams. Differs from older systems such as Imperial or US customary units.

The Meter

The base unit of length in the metric system. Defined originally as 1/10,000,000 of the distance from equator to North Pole, now defined using the speed of light.

Traditional US Units

Still used in everyday life in the US: inches, feet, pounds. Scientific measurements prefer metric units for consistency.
